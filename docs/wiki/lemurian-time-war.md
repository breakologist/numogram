# Lemurian Time War

Sourced summary (Em, 1 Nov 2026) of the Ccru text *Lemurian Time War* [ccru.net, [archive/burroughs.htm](http://www.ccru.net/archive/burroughs.htm)]. The ccru.net archive index lists it as "William Kaye on William S. Burroughs", in *William Burroughs and the Global Order* (Pluto Press, 2004). It is also reprinted in *Ccru: Writings 1997–2003*. The text is paraphrased below; only brief phrases are quoted.

## Frame
- The account is based on "sensitive information" from an informant Ccru calls **William Kaye**. Ccru first met him on 20 March 1999, and his papers reached Ccru in January 2000 (see [[book-of-paths]]).
- Ccru says the account is "partially fictionalized" to protect him, calls much of it "extremely implausible", and admits Kaye was prone to "paranoid-chronomaniac hallucination". Fact and fiction are deliberately mixed.
- Kaye's employer was **Peter Vysparov**, and his organisation was "The Order", or, after Burroughs, "**The Board**". The Board was born from a nightmare of time "spiraling out of control". It hates spirals because they have loose ends. It assigned Kaye to terminate the "**spiral templex**", a concept attributed to R. E. Templeton's Miskatonic lectures and summarised in *The Templeton Episode*.

## Kaye's central claim
- Burroughs's story *The Ghost Lemurs of Madagascar* (commissioned and published by *Omni*, 1987) decisively shaped the career of the pirate **Captain Mission**, founder of Libertatia on Madagascar about 1700, three centuries earlier.
- Kaye said the Vysparov library held an old illustrated copy in Mission's hand. The copy dated itself "Now, in 1987" and had been catalogued there since 1789.

## Hyperstition
- Vysparov convened the **Cthulhu Club** in the postwar years to study the links between Lovecraft's fiction, myth, science and magic. He was then setting up **Miskatonic Virtual University (MVU)**.
- **Hyperstition** was debated from the club's start and is loosely defined as "fictions that make themselves real". Kaye pointed to a line in Burroughs's *Ah Pook is Here* about a virus as a sequence of glyphs "that makes itself real".
- Hyperstition is set against postmodern scepticism. It is not "nothing is real" but a matter of **degrees of realisation**. Its stance is "positive unbelief". Writing works as magic (signs that change reality), and fictions-within-fictions act as a "Chinese box" for smuggling sorcery past Control.

## The One God Universe (OGU) and time
- OGU is Burroughs's name for the ruling control programme. It is monotheistic, prerecorded, and wins by denying that magical war exists. Its rival is MU, the **Magical Universe** of many gods.
- The key link is between control and time: "Control needs time", and in the end "the One God is Time". Korzybski's "time-binding" takes on a sinister second meaning.
- The **cut-ups and fold-ins** (Gysin's role is acknowledged) are read as "time-war tactics" against the prerecorded. One fold-in describes page 1 folded into page 100 and inserted as page 10, a jump across decimal magnitudes (note 10).

## The episode (1958) and the Rift (1987)
- Vysparov met Burroughs in Paris in summer **1958**. Kaye met Burroughs at Vysparov's New York library on **23 December 1958**. Ccru notes it found **no evidence** of a US trip that year.
- In the library Burroughs found *The Ghost Lemurs of Madagascar*, a book he had not yet written, and fell into a trance. Kaye links this to Burroughs's later visions, his sense of a double, and the urgency of the cut-ups.
- **1987** is the "eye" of the spiral templex. In that year Burroughs visited the Duke University Lemur Center, was writing *The Western Lands*, and published *Ghost Lemurs* in *Omni*. Lemurs (the word is glossed as "ghost") become door-keepers back to Lemuria and to Mission.
- The Board tried to make Burroughs a "dead end", sealing the loop by "White Chronomancy" (closing runaway time inside closed loops). Burroughs instead rewrote it as *The Ghost of Chance* (1991), which reopened the way to the Western Lands.
- Kaye's last note speaks of termination becoming confused with the eddies of a latent spiral current.

## Notes Ccru adds (selected)
- Lemuria comes from Sclater and Haeckel's hypothetical lost continent, which plate tectonics later displaced. It was then taken up by occultists (Blavatsky, Scott-Elliot, Steiner) and merged with Mu.
- Ccru corrects Burroughs's figure of 160 million years: lemurs are about 55 million years old, and Madagascar split from Africa about 120 million years ago.
- Vysparov's treatise *Atlantean Black Magic* (Kingsport Press, 1949) links him to Crowley and the OTO.
- Kaye pointed out that "Refuge" is number 666 in Roget's Thesaurus.

## What the text does **not** contain
The text makes **no** mention of AxSys, Templars or the Numogram's zones. Its only numbers are dates, 666 (Roget) and the 1/10/100 fold-in. Any numogram mapping is interpretation.
