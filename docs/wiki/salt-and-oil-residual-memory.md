**Classification:** Residual Narrative Echo / Environmental Persistence Artifact

Certain environments retain informational weight after the initiating actors have departed.

Oil is not flame. Oil is possibility awaiting activation.

**Ontological analogue:** Oil maps onto FOOM's `U` (Uncertainty) slot — the space where compression is failing, where varentropy lives. It is the cost the numogram cannot compress away.[^foom-semiodynamics-compression]
