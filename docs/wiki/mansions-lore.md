# Lunar mansions and decans: lore

This page is a companion to [[lunation-and-mansions]], which documents the computations. It collects what the traditions *say* about each mansion and decan. Sourced material comes first; the speculative section is labelled.

## Sourced

### 1. Chinese 28 xiu 宿

The xiu are unequal, sidereal and measured on the equator. Each one starts at its **determinative star** 距星. The xiu are grouped into the **Four Symbols** 四象.

- **Almanac use:** each xiu is paired with one of the seven luminaries (七曜) and an animal; the pairing is called 禽星. The tongshu also gives each xiu a 吉 (good) or 凶 (bad) verdict.
- **Important:** the almanac's "宿直" day-lodge is a fixed 28-day count tied to the weekday. It is *not* the Moon's actual position (zh.wikipedia 二十八宿, §術數).

- **The two Wikipedias disagree** for 心 (σ vs α Sco), 奎 (η vs ζ And), 觜 and 參. Zh.wiki gives the Han–Yuan determinatives, plus the 1752 swap, when 觜 became λ Ori and 參 became ζ Ori to restore 觜-before-參 after precession inverted them. The code uses σ Sco, η And, λ Ori and ζ Ori.
- **Older determinatives:** on the early-Han Ruyin-hou disc, the determinatives were the bright stars: **Antares** for 心, **Aldebaran** for 畢 and **Betelgeuse** for 參 (zh.wiki §星宿的調整).
- **Bi 壁 (today).** The "eastern wall" (東壁) of the Encampment square. The almanac verse quoted at sldtswh.com and yixiansheng.com reads "壁星造作主增財", roughly "construction under Bi brings wealth". The listed good activities are weddings, building and burial (宜: 婚礼、建造、埋葬). The listed avoidance is travel south (忌: 往南方).
- **The almanac's own lodge today is a different xiu.** Its day-count is not lunar. From one anchor (shunshi.ai gives 2026-08-23 = 房) and the 28-day count, 24 Oct 2026 is **女土蝠 (凶)**. That agrees with Saturday = 土. [approx, one anchor]

### 2. Arabic 28 manāzil (Agrippa II.33, from Picatrix I.4)

Agrippa's text is the 1651 English translation, which is public domain (https://www.esotericarchives.com/agrippa/agripp2c.htm). He says the mansions are "fixed in the eight sphere" and that the first begins "from the head of Aries of the eighth sphere". In other words they are **sidereal**. Each is 12°51′26″ wide, "according to the opinion of Abraham". The calendar now uses sidereal Lahiri as the primary reading and gives the tropical one as an alternative. It is a good sign that the stars land in their namesakes this way: the Pleiades fall in 3 Athoray ("Pleiades"), Aldebaran in 4 Aldebaram, Spica in 14 Achureth ("spike of Virgo") and Antares in 18 Alchas ("Heart of Scorpio").

Agrippa's attributions follow, quoted or closely summarised from the public-domain 1651 text. The Arabic names in brackets are Peterson's notes on that page.

1. Alnath (al-Sharatan), horns of Aries: "causeth discords, and journies".
2. Allothaim (al-Butain), belly of Aries: finding treasures, retaining captives.
3. Achaomazon / Athoray (al-Thurayya), **the Pleiades**: "profitable to Saylors, Huntsmen, and Alchymists".
4. Aldebaram (al-Dabaran), eye of Taurus: hinders buildings, fountains, wells and mines; "begetteth discord".
5. Alchatay (al-Haq'a): return from journeys, instruction of scholars, confirms buildings, health and good will.
6. Alhanna (al-Han'a): hunting, sieges, revenge of princes; destroys harvests, hinders physicians.
7. Aldimiach (al-Dhira'), arm of Gemini: gain, friendship, lovers; scares flies; "destroyeth Magisteries".
8. Alnaza (al-Nathra), misty: love, friendship, fellow travellers; drives away mice; confirms imprisonment.
9. Archaam (al-Tarf), eye of the Lion: hinders harvests and travellers; discord.
10. Algelioche (al-Jabha), forehead of Leo (**Regulus**): strengthens buildings, love and benevolence, help against enemies.
11. Azobra (al-Zubra): voyages, merchandise, redeeming captives.
12. Alzarpha (al-Sarfa), tail of Leo: harvests and plantations; hinders seamen; good for servants and captives.
13. Alhaire (al-'Awwa'): benevolence, gain, voyages, harvests, freeing captives.
14. Achureth (al-Simak), **Spica**: "cureth the sick", good for sailors, hinders land journeys.
15. Agrapha (al-Ghafr): extracting treasures, digging wells; divorce, discord, destroying houses and enemies.
16. Azubene (al-Zubana), horns of Scorpio: hinders journeys, wedlock, harvests and merchandise; helps redeem captives.
17. Alchil (al-Iklil), crown of Scorpio: "bettereth a bad fortune, maketh love durable", strengthens buildings, helps seamen.
18. Alchas (al-Qalb), **Heart of Scorpio (Antares)**: discord, sedition, conspiracy against princes; frees captives, helps buildings.
19. Allatha (al-Shawla), tail of Scorpio: sieges, taking towns, driving men from their places, harm to seamen and captives.
20. Abnahaya (al-Na'a'im): taming wild beasts, strengthening prisons; compels a man to come to a place.
21. Abeda (al-Balda), desert: harvests, gain, buildings, travellers; divorce.
22. Sadahacha (Sa'd al-Dhabih): flight of servants and captives; curing diseases.
23. Zabadola (Sa'd Bula'), swallowing: divorce, liberty of captives, health of the sick.
24. Sadabath (Sa'd al-Su'ud), "the Star of fortune": love of married folk, victory of soldiers; hurts government.
25. Sadalabra / Sadalachia (Sa'd al-Akhbiya): sieges, revenge, divorce; confirms prisons; binding spells.
26. Alpharg (al-Fargh al-Muqaddam), first drawing: union and love; health of captives; destroys prisons.
27. Alcharya (al-Fargh al-Mu'akhkhar), second drawing: harvests, revenues, healing; hinders buildings; danger to seamen.
28. **Albotham (al-Risha'), Pisces**: "encreaseth Harvests and Merchandize, it secureth travellers through dangerous places; it maketh for the joy of marryed couples, but it strengthenth prisons, and causeth loss of treasures".

Agrippa adds that the ancients gave each mansion "his resemblances, Images, and seals, and his president intelligences". Those images are in Picatrix and are not reproduced here; see Flags.

### 3. Nakshatras (brief)

Today's are 27 **Revati** ("wealthy"), whose deity is Pushan, the nourisher and protector of travellers and herds, and before that 26 **Uttara Bhadrapada**, under Ahir Budhnya, the serpent of the deep. Both are listed on en.wikipedia "Nakshatra" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakshatra).

- **Orion** is the core of 參 Shen ("Three Stars", the Belt). Its head λ Ori is 觜. Betelgeuse 參宿四 and Rigel 參宿七 belong to 參. Agrippa II.37: "Orion granteth victory".
- **Sirius** is not a xiu. It is the asterism 天狼 inside the 井 Well region. In the Shiji's older Gan De-style list, 狼 was one of the lodges (zh.wiki §星宿的調整).
- **Polaris** belongs to the 紫微垣, the Purple Forbidden Enclosure, one of the three enclosures 三垣 around the pole. It is not a mansion. Right-ascension bands are meaningless that close to the pole.
- **Sources:** the Chinese star names for the Behenian stars (畢宿五, 心宿二, 天狼, 軒轅十四, 大角, 織女一, 北落師門 and so on) are the standard modern names listed on each star's Wikipedia page. They were not re-checked one by one this session; see Flags.

### 5. Decans: Agrippa II.37, now in the calendar

`--decan` now prints Agrippa's image for the Sun's decan. The images are from the 1651 public-domain English, stored in `cli/calendar/data/agrippa_decans.json` and slightly abridged. Rulers stay in Chaldean order from Mars at Aries I, as in the existing code. Agrippa credits the images to "Teucer the Babylonian … after whom the Arabians also wrote".

**Today, Scorpio I** (Mars; the 5 of Cups): "a woman of good face and habit, and two men striking her; the operations of these are for comliness, beauty, and for strifes, treacheries, deceits, detractations, and perditions."

**Picatrix II.11** gives a parallel set of 36 decan images (*Ghayat al-Hakim*; Latin ed. Pingree 1986). The English translations by Greer and Warnock (2010/2011) and by Attrell and Porreca (2019) are in copyright. I did not have a verified text this session, so none is quoted or summarised here. That is left as a follow-up rather than reconstructed from memory.

## Speculative (Em, not sourced)

*Interpretation only.* Wall (壁, the library and archive xiu in later star lore) sits beside the sovereign hexagram 23 Bo, "Splitting Apart", and Wen's First Frost line 58.3: walls going up while the house is stripped. Albotham closes the sidereal round of mansions and Revati closes the nakshatras, a run of last mansions just before the Hunter's full moon. These are associations, not claims. Note too that the choice between tropical and sidereal flips Agrippa's verdict, from "discords" (Alnath) to "safety of travellers" (Albotham).

## Flags

- **Corrected today:** the calendar first treated Agrippa's manāzil as tropical. His text says "eighth sphere", which means sidereal, so the default is now sidereal (Lahiri) and tropical is the alternative.
- **Determinatives disagree** between en.wiki and zh.wiki (心, 奎, 觜, 參). The values the code uses are listed in the table.
- **Almanac caveats:** the 吉/凶 verdicts are the common tongshu formula. Detailed do/avoid lists vary between almanacs and are quoted from one family of web transcriptions (sldtswh.com and yixiansheng.com), not a printed tongshu.
- **Chinese star names** for the Behenian stars were not individually verified.
- **Picatrix decan and mansion images** are not included because the English translations are copyrighted and no verified text was available.
