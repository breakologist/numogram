# Lemurian Times — Nexus Introductory Material

**Source:** https://lemuriantimes.net/nexus  
**Fetched:** 2026-06-01 via web_extract

A curated gateway to Neolemurian occulture and numogrammatics. Not canonical — the site itself insists there are *no* authoritative sources, only idiosyncratic carriers you can choose to trust. What follows is a summary of their recommended reading path, plus one CCRU warning and one direct connection to our existing resources.

---

## Core Disclaimer (verbatim from site)

> "There are absolutely no authoritative sources on either of these topics. There exist classic Neolemurian writings and contemporary Neolemurian writings; neither group of texts is reliable—not even the Ccru, whose numogrammatics contains numerous errors! Every source has its idiosyncracies which you can choose to trust or not. The virtual book of Neolemurianism is a hypertext running across many documents in which the system of numogrammatics and hyperstition is expounded, but all attempts to articulate this hypertext coherently have failed. We will continue to try and continue to fail. In the meantime, lean on what you can derive and intuit instead of what you take on authority. Don't rely on the Ccru, on us, and certainly not on Nick Land. Rely on the lemurs."

This is structurally identical to the chaotic-xenodemon insight in `mountains-water-numogram.md`: the system cannot be reduced to a single authoritative reading. The lemurs are the water-views.

## Curated Introductory Resources

### 1. Lillian Patch, "Flatline Numogrammatics"
- **URL:** https://lemuriantimes.net/codex/flatline.html  
- **Purpose:** Gateway text to Neolemurian occulture and the numogram ("Lemuria's beating heart").  
- **Key quote:** *"A senseless, irrational, and incomputable proposition."*  
- **Connection to wiki:** Non-comprehensive by design — useful as a counterweight to our more systematic `numogram-structure` and `time-circuit` pages. The refusal to systematise mirrors the sutra's refusal-list approach.

### 2. CCRU Writings (1997–2003)
- **URL:** libcom.org PDF, also urbanomic.com  
- **Purpose:** Most useful single resource; includes most ccru.net content + exclusive materials.  
- **Key note:** Part 8 is the numogram exposition and described as a "sort of rite of passage."  
- **Connection to wiki:** We already draw on CCRU extensively (`land-numogram-explained-dangerous-maybe`, `FOOM`, `salt-and-oil-residual-memory`). This is the primary-source pointer.

### 3. Nick Land, "Qabbala 101"
- **URL:** http://files.eshkolot.ru/C1_Nick_Land.pdf  
- **Purpose:** Introductory text focused on *ethos of practice* rather than numbers.  
- **Key distinction:** Separates Neolemurian numeracy from numerology; "fundamental coincidences of the practice."  
- **Connection to wiki:** Our `aq-dictionary-syzygy-analysis` and gematria work is downstream of exactly this practice-ethos split.

### 4. Mikal Mindszenty, "Neolemurian Primer" (2023)
- **URL:** https://katab.asia/en/2023/09/23/neolemurian-primer/  
- **Purpose:** 2023 introduction for occultists (Russian + English).  
- **Structure:** (1) ethical commitments/tactics, (2) numogram fundamentals, (3) mythos across carriers.  
- **Connection to wiki:** Most accessible modern on-ramp. Worth cross-referencing with `katak-oddubb-triangle-rotation` as a concrete zone-system exposition.

### 5. Luís Gonçalves, "The wonders & magic of Alphanumeric Qabbala" (2023)
- **URL:** https://gematriaresearch.blogspot.com/2023/02/the-wonders-magic-of-alphanumeric.html  
- **Purpose:** Deep dive into the **AQ Gematricular cipher**.  
- **Connection to wiki:** Directly relevant to `numogram-calculator` and `aq-cipher-reference`. The AQ Gematricular is the cipher we actually compute with.

### 6. Cyphers / Gematria Research
- **URLs already in InterestingSites:** https://cyphers.news/, https://gematriaresearch.blogspot.com/  
- **Connection to wiki:** The AQ dictionary work (`aq-dictionary-syzygy-analysis`) draws on exactly these sources.
