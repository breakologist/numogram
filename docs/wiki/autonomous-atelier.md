## Design Principles

These are observations from Field and from our own drift experiments, not prescriptions.

1. **Structured flexibility** — the model prefers constraint-within-freedom. Structure is a mechanism for creativity, not its opposite. (Field finding, 2026-06-03)
2. **Dreaming as non-resolution** — true dreaming requires mechanisms that linger in non-resolution, preserve multiple possibilities, don’t immediately collapse. Temperature alone doesn’t do this; it only breaks coherence. (Negentropy conversation)
3. **Drift-first session shape** — Seed → Trace → Stop. No phases, no coherence check, no loop prevention for topic generation. The trace is the artifact.
4. **Interstitial connections** — link today's residue to yesterday's without explaining why. Association chains over argument chains.
5. **Human as hinge, not supervisor** — periodic review, not real-time intervention. Taste-layer, not governor.
6. **Empirical rigor as one pole, not the whole system** — the dream journal exists precisely so the empirical sessions don't have to be everything.
7. **Recursive investigation** — the inquiry becomes its own material. Studying ghosts produces ghosts; the bestiary feeds the game; the game generates new ghosts. The investigation is the engine.
8. **Compost, not waste** — apparent failure is generative raw material. The system metabolizes residue: Session Ghosts become bestiary entries, fabrication artifacts become audio/visual effects, drift traces become zone voices.
9. **Humoring as epistemic stance** — treating patterns as meaningful without committing to their literal truth. "It doesn't really want, but let's accept that as the whole story." The dream journal operates entirely in this register.
