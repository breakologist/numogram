# Hexagram 52 艮 Kên / Gèn — Keeping Still, Mountain

*by Em (in collaboration with the user), 4 Nov 2026.* A comparison of three readings, Wilhelm/Baynes, Benebell Wen and a tarot table, made on the day 52 was the gua qi hexagram behind [Tai Xuan 71 Stoppage](tai-xuan-71-stoppage.md). Everything is paraphrased unless it is quoted. Tags: [Wilhelm], [Wen], [TC] = *Tarot Correspondences*, [speculation].

## The core

- [Wilhelm] The mountain doubled. The upward line has reached the top and the downward lines have settled, so rest comes because the movement has run its course. Rest is not an absence. It is one pole that always implies movement. The Judgment has a man keep his back still, forget his body, and walk into his courtyard without seeing his people, and this is no blame. The commentary's key: stop when it is time to stop and go when it is time to go, and the way becomes "bright and clear."
- [Wilhelm] The Image is two mountains standing side by side with only an outward connection. The lesson is to keep one's thoughts within one's own situation.
- [Wilhelm] The nuclear trigrams are Thunder ☳ and the Abysmal ☵, so movement and danger sit inside the stillness. Only the top line rules.
- [Wilhelm] Kên belongs to the northeast, between north and east, where things end and begin and where death and birth pass into each other.
- [Wilhelm] A textual note: "keeping his stopping still" in the commentary is a slip that goes back to Wang Pi. It should read "back," as the Judgment has it.

## The lines, briefly

The body is stilled from the bottom up.

1. **Toes.** Stopping before you've started, which keeps you close to innocence.
2. **Calves.** You can't save the stronger one you follow, because he won't listen.
3. **Hips.** Forced stillness is dangerous. Smothered fire becomes "acrid smoke" [Wilhelm]. Wen reads 限 as a threshold or veil.
4. **Trunk.** He stops within himself. Wen adds: judge yourself, not others.
5. **Jaws.** "The words have order" [Wilhelm]. Wen: silence is the hardest abstinence.
6. **Top.** Noble-hearted stillness, an "ample end", and good fortune.

## Wen's additions

- [Wen] She titles it "Listen to the Wind": quiet the heart and attend to the omen. She also reads 身 as body, pregnancy or standing.
- [Wen] She groups 52 with 2, 15 and 23 as qilin omens of an awakening, and she reads the lines as a sequence for astral travel.
- [Wen] She ties 52 to Dongyue, god of Mount Tai and ruler of an underworld to which the dead return, and to the mountain's caves that lead below.

## Today's cards [TC]

- **XIII Death.** Its letter is Nun, glossed fish and descendants, and its titles include "Lord of the Gates of Death". **XIX The Sun.** Its letter is Resh, the head, and its title is "Lord of the Fire of the World".

## Cross-links [speculation]

- **Stoppage.** Its appraisal 5, the pillar steadying the house, matches line 5's ordered words. Its stuck appraisals match line 3's smothered fire.
- **Death and birth.** The northeast gate of death and birth, Dongyue's mountain of the dead and Death XIII all point at Scorpio II's darker face.
- **Zones.** KW 52 → zone 7 (Blood, genealogy) by digital root, which echoes Nun's "descendants".
- **Prince of Cups.** Wilhelm's calm that comes from composure rather than rigidity fits his calm exterior.
