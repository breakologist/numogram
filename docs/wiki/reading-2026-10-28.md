## The head question

**Sourced**
- Hexagram 23 has five broken lines under one unbroken line at the top. It is a house whose roof is the only thing still standing.
- **Wilhelm/Baynes** (Princeton edn, ISBN 0691284571):
  - The top line is "a large fruit still uneaten". When the fruit falls, its seed grows again.
  - The Judgement says it does not further one to go anywhere. The time calls for submitting and waiting, not for acting.
  - The Image has those above making their position secure through generosity to those below.
- In Jarry's calendar, the Chapeau (hat) of Bosse-de-Nage comes the day after his Résurrection.

**Speculative**
- *(The user's question.)* In Splitting Apart only the top strong line remains, like the hat that holds the baboon together. Should things be allowed to split, or does the head have the right idea?
- *(Em's reading.)* The surviving line is a seed, not a defence against decay. It does not stop the splitting: it is what stays whole so that renewal can happen afterwards. That fits a resurrection followed by a hat. The failure mode is a head that clings, trying to hold the house up instead of letting the fruit drop. The Image's head secures itself by giving downwards, not by gripping.
- *(Em, speculative.)* Ubu, on Terrasse's day, is the counter-image: a grotesque head that seizes the whole state and feeds on it ("Phynances").
- *(The user's addition.)* 29 October may be the point where the head turns things round.

## The 5 of Cups and Severance

**Sourced**
- In the Golden Dawn (Book T), the 5 of Cups is "Lord of Loss in Pleasure", Mars in Scorpio. Crowley calls it "Disappointment".
- In the wiki's numogram tables, **4::5** is the syzygy of the demon **Katak**, and it feeds the **Sink** current (4::5 → 1). **3::6** is the **Warp** syzygy and **2::7** feeds the **Hold** current.

**Speculative (Em)**
- The usual picture of the card is spilled cups with some still standing. That is the same shape as Splitting Apart: most has gone, something remains.
- The decan (zone 4) and the hexagram (zone 5) together make the 4::5 syzygy: the Sink, Katak's drop.
- If Severance is placed at zone 6 or 7, it pairs with 3 or 2 (Warp 3::6, Hold 2::7). That pulls away from the Sink.
- The question it leaves: cut the thread cleanly, or let it fray?

## Continued: 30–31 October

**Sourced**
- **Friday 30 October:** the 'pataphysical day is St J.-P. Brisset, philologue, prince des penseurs. Jean-Pierre Brisset argued, through chains of puns, that humans descend from frogs.
- **Tai Xuan:** 70 Severance, line 6. The gua qi of hexagram 23 Splitting Apart continues.
- **Saturday 31 October:** Nos Galan Gaeaf, the Welsh eve of winter. A folk rhyme warns that the *hwch ddu gwta* (tailless black sow) and the *ladi wen heb ben* (headless white lady) will catch whoever is last home from the bonfire.
- **'Pataphysical calendar:** Commémoration du Cure-dent.
- All Saints follows on 1 November.

**Speculative (Em's, endorsed by the user)**
- The mouth thread returns through Brisset, with the Tower's letter Pe as mouth. Puns split words apart and recombine them. That is an image for Severance as a splitting and recombining of sense, rather than pure loss.
- The headless lady extends the head question. It is a night that dreads losing one's head, set against Bosse-de-Nage's hat and the "uneaten fruit" head of Splitting Apart.

## Continued: 1 November (Calan Gaeaf, All Saints)

**Sourced**
- **Sunday 1 November 2026:** All Saints' Day. In Wales, Calan Gaeaf, the first day of winter.
- **'Pataphysical calendar:** the Occultation d'Alfred Jarry. Jarry died in Paris on 1 November 1907, at the Hôpital de la Charité, of tubercular meningitis.
- **Tai Xuan:** 71 Stoppage, line 1. **Gua qi:** hexagram 23 Splitting Apart continues.
- **Wilhelm/Baynes** (Princeton edn, ISBN 0691284571, Book I pp. 93–96; read from the user's photographs):
  - The hexagram belongs to "the ninth month (October–November)".
  - The lines form a house, the top line its roof; the roof "is being shattered" and the house collapses.
  - The bed imagery rises line by line: the leg, then the edge, then "up to the skin", where the disaster reaches the occupant as well as the resting place.
  - Line 3: "He splits with them. No blame."
  - Line 5: the dark line is "the head of the other weak lines", leading them to the strong line like "a shoal of fishes".
  - Top line commentary: evil "lives solely by negation" and so destroys itself.

**Speculative (Em's, endorsed by the user)**
- The shattered house echoes XVI The Tower, falling in its own calendar month.
- The bed that reaches the occupant sits uncannily with Jarry dying in bed of an illness of the head on this date. As the user noted, the Occultation matches the hexagram.
- Line 5's head leading the many to the one reads as an All Saints procession: another head in the week's head thread.
- Line 3 answers the Severance question from 28 October: a clean break, not fraying.
- Evil living by negation is a gloss on Ubu, the grotesque head that devours itself.
