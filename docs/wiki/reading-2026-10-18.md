How the zones are computed (from `cli/calendar/README.md`):

- **Decan zone 3.** This is the digital root of the decan number. Libra III is decan 21, and 2 + 1 = 3.
- **Nma zone 2.** This is the digital root of the day index. 101202₃ = 243 + 27 + 18 + 2 = 290, and 290 → 11 → 2. The display label reads A-291. The README marks the Nma zone rule as [spec], and CCRU gives no epoch: the default epoch, 1970-01-01, follows the xeno.cx reconstruction. So the Nma zone is the weakest of the day's numbers.
- **Tai Xuan zone 4.** In the calendar this is the digital root of the head number, 67 → 13 → 4.
- **Gua qi.** Meng Xi's hexagram for the date is 36 Ming Yi, in the solar term 寒露 (Cold Dew).

## Tetragram 67 — Darkening

- **Lines** (bottom → top): Heaven, Earth, Earth, Man. `represent_tetragram(66)` returns `[0, 1, 1, 2]`.
- **Base-3 value:** 0·1 + 1·3 + 1·9 + 2·27 = 66.
- **Zones:** [[zone-3]] by line value (`--zone-from value`, the CLI default) and [[zone-4]] by head number 67.
- **Why the top two lines don't count.** The line weights are 1, 3, 9 and 27, and 9 ≡ 27 ≡ 0 (mod 9). So only the bottom two lines (Heaven, Earth → 0 + 3 = 3) set the zone. The top line is the Man line, the project's "Em" (see the note in [[tai-hsuan-ching]] and [[em-state-analysis]]). The numogram cannot see the mediating line at all.
- Value zone and number zone always differ by one, on all 81 figures. See "Two clocks" in [[numogram-tsubuyaki-off-by-one]].

## Hexagram 36 — Ming Yi, "Darkening of the Light"

- **Figure:** Fire (☲) under Earth (☷). The yang lines are lines 1 and 3, with weights 1 and 4.
- **Line value:** 1 + 4 = 5, so it is [[zone-5]] by lines (`fuxi`, the default). By King Wen number, 36 → [[zone-9]]. [[hexagram-zone-mapping]] lists 36 with value 5, zone 5, syzygy 4::5 and Katak.
- **Two darkenings.** The calendar happens to pair the tetragram *Darkening* with the hexagram *Darkening of the Light*. Their zones split two ways: 3 | 4 for the tetragram and 5 | 9 for the hexagram.

## Gate structure: why there is no road into 4

- **Gate rule.** Gate *n* sends zone *n* to the digital root of T(*n*) = *n*(*n*+1)/2. For zones 1–9 the targets are 1, 3, 6, 1, 6, 3, 1, 9, 9. **Only 1, 3, 6 and 9 ever receive a gate, so no gate lands on zone 4.** See [[gate-mechanics]].
- **The route out of 4.**
  - 4 → 5 by the Sink syzygy (4::5; see [[syzygy]]).
  - 5 → 6 by Gt-15 (T(5) = 15 → 6).
  - 6 → 3 by Gt-21, then 3 → 6 by Gt-6, and so on round the [[numogram-warp|Warp]].
- **No return.** Once in 3 ↔ 6, gates, syzygy and current all stay inside the pair. This is the Warp vortex of [[zone-3]], drawn as "Katak descent" in [[numogram-tsubuyaki-off-by-one]].
- So today's tetragram, read by number (zone 4), can only flow *into* its own reading by value (zone 3). The reverse move does not exist.

## Decan: Libra III, 4 of Swords

The Sun is at 25° Libra, in the third decan, ruled by Jupiter (Chaldean order). The element is Air and the Golden Dawn *Book T* card is the 4 of Swords. The calendar shows XI Justice for the sign and X Wheel of Fortune for the ruler. The decan zone is 3. ## St Luke (feast)

The [[st-luke-golden-legend|Golden Legend]] chapter (Caxton/Ellis, Temple Classics 1900; [Fordham etext](https://sourcebooks.fordham.edu/basis/goldenlegend/GoldenLegend-Volume6.asp)) opens with a light etymology: "Luke is as much to say as arising or enhancing himself. Or Luke is said of light". It calls him "light of the world".

- **Light against Darkening** *(speculative)*. The saint whose name *is* light falls on the day of tetragram 67 *Darkening*. The calendar does not choose this, but it puts the two side by side.
- **Hidden light against Ming Yi** *(speculative)*. In the chapter, Ezekiel's book becomes Luke's gospel, "written within for to hide the mystery of profoundness, and without for the showing of the history". Ming Yi's image is light gone under the earth, still burning but concealed. The gospel holds an inside that hides and an outside that shows, as Fire sits inside Earth.
- **Caveats.** The chapter has no painter story, death or relics. The Welsh links are loose. The St Luke's Little Summer details come from search snippets. All of this is set out on [[st-luke-golden-legend]].

## The user's observations

These are recorded as the user offered them. All are *(speculative)*.

- **St Luke and light against the darkness hexagrams.** Luke's feast meets *two* darkening figures, 67 and 36, and the saint's name is light.
- **The 4 of Swords as ideas in stability.** Swords are the suit of Air and intellect, and four is the number of the square. The card reads as thought held still, ideas resting in a stable form rather than in conflict. In the chapter, Luke's whole portrait is a four-square figure: four faces and four "ordinances".
- **Jupiter in Libra feels social.** The decan ruler is expansive Jupiter in the sign of the scales and of partnership. That suits a saint described as Paul's constant companion ("Luke is only with me") who lived "companionably".
- **Zone 3 against zone 4: two states at once, or a movement.** Darkening is zone 3 and zone 4 simultaneously, depending on which count you trust. Or it is a *movement* from 4 to 3. The gate structure supports the second reading as a one-way passage: 4 → 5 → 6 → 3 and then no way back. Read as a movement, the day starts in the Sink, catastrophe and stillness, and ends in the Warp. Read as two states, it holds both at once, like the 4 of Swords' rest inside the suit of strife.
