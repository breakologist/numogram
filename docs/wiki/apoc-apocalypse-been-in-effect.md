# Apocalypse been in Effect?

> *Maria de Rosario reports on "hyperfictional" and "ethnomathematical" studies of strange goings-on on the Web, and asks: Are the Cyber-Time Cults just the latest craze, or a new Millennial Mythology?*

**Original:** *New England Educational Review*, August 1998.  
**CCRU path:** `syzygy/apoc.htm` (rendered at `http://127.0.0.1:8080/syzygy/apoc.htm`).

## 1. Cybergoths as Cargo-Cult

> "The Cybergoths are a Cargo-Cult. They integrate technology into their belief system, and transform the past into the future. Or vice versa." — Dr Linda Trent, Miskatonic University

- Like Melanesian syncretic cults turning Western detritus into sacred objects, Cybergoths use **information-age relics** as objects of a new mythic system.
- Trent interjects: *"It's wrong, strictly speaking, to describe the system as belonging to the Cybergoths. They use it, but its origins are very mysterious."*

## 2. Hyperstition (coined here)

> "It's not a simple matter of true or false with hyperstitious systems. Belief here doesn't have a simply passive quality. The situation is closer to the modern phenomenon of hype than to religious belief as we'd ordinarily think about it. Hype actually makes things happen, and uses belief as a positive power. Just because it's not 'real' now, doesn't mean it won't be real at some point in the future. And once it's real, in a sense, it's always been."

**Trent's definition is the canonical coinage.** This is the primary source for the term as used throughout the wiki.

## 3. The Millennium Bug as Gregorian Restoration

- K-Goths (substituting K for cyber-) believe the Y2K fix is a **Gregorian Restoration** program.
- Cyberspace already has a calendar: 00 = 1900 → 99 = 1999.
- To avoid 2000 = 00 = 1900, the K-Goths advocate **continuing the existing K-calendar** by adding *one* extra digit, not two.
- They will "chill out" to year **K-100** rather than celebrate 2000.

## 4. Hyper-C: The Other Time War Faction

- **Hyper-C** is an ultra-secret web movement with even weirder chronopolitics than the K-Goths.
- Celebrate the *return* that the K-calendar tries to avoid: computers should not be tampered with; the Millennium Time-Bomb will explode western chronology.
- Pun on sea + C: **Hypersea** (biological theory that "life on land" extends the ocean) + Century + Cybernetics + Cycle.
- Shady ops woven into **eso-terrorism** conducted through **sonic fiction** (Kodwo Eshun's term).
- **Drexciya** cited as example: marine species descended from pregnant African slaves thrown overboard — babies born at sea who never needed air. Eshun: *"The mutation of the African male and female slaves in the 18th century into what became negro, and into the entire series of humans that were designed in America."*
- Eshun's hypersitious network includes: **Sun Ra, Public Enemy, George Clinton, Underground Resistance**.
- Central theme: **abduction** — *"the idea of alien abduction means that we've all been living in an alien-nation since the 18th century."*

## 5. The Time War

> "The struggle between Hyper-C and the Cybergoths is part of an ongoing 'time war' ('a war that's only going to get worse, and which will affect all of us')."

- Cybergoths have an **"extremely sophisticated" philosophy of time** using two interrelated diagram-graphizations:
  1. The **Barker Spiral**
  2. The **Stillwell Numogram** (which includes the Pentazygon)

## 6. The Barker Spiral & Numogram (from Dr Polly Wolfe)

> "The Barker spiral maps a simple set of numeric relations, but these relations have enormously complex implications."

- One side: all numbers add to 10 (1+9, 2+8, 3+7, 4+6, 5+5)
- Other, "occulted" side: 10 is subtracted; numbers twinned to make 9 (0+9, 1+8, 2+7, 3+6, 4+5)
- **Digital reduction** as key operation: any number reduces to 1–9; 9 functions as 0.
- Numogram = elaboration of the Spiral: combines digital reduction with **triangular numbering**.
- Dr Polly Wolfe: *"I'm an Aftermathematician. I like to see how numbers behave; I'm not interested in reducing them to instantiations of some pre-existing logical system."*
- **Mechanomics**: numeric dabbling is not representational but practical. *"Making things up is always a matter of subtracting from zero."*
- **Gothic numerics**: infinitesimals as major theme; zero itself is a Gothic quantity; **Uttunul** is connected with Cantorian continuum.

## 7. The Pentazygon (Five Syzygy Entities)

| Syzygy | Entity | Character |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| 1::8 | **Mur Mur** | Dreaming demon of submersion. Echoes of sea beasts and ancient serpents. Deep Time of the ocean bed. Horrified by Oddubb's division of undivided waters. |
| 5::4 | **Katak** | Associated with desert, heat haze, shimmer. Claw marks, teeth. Time of cataclysm; appearance presages disaster. Hydrophobic / rabid dog. Horrified by Mur Mur. |
| 2::7 | **Oddubb** | Amphibious entity, crossing out of water, acquisition of lungs. *"Double-agency,"* duplicitous. Defined by ambiguous and elusive movement. Horrified by dryness, by being fully landlocked (Katak). |
| 3::6 | **Djynxx** | *"Changeling figure, defined by a jinking (erratic or zig-zagging) movement, a sudden cutting in or out."* Outside sequential time. Associated with old mythologies of child abduction and Hell. |
| 9::0 | **Uttunul** | *"Flatline" entity. Continuum, zero-intensity, void — eternity not as infinitely extended time, but as **No-Time**.* Outside sequential time. Most fascinating and disturbing. |

- First three (Mur Mur, Katak, Oddubb) = **cycle of time**.
- Last two (Djynxx, Uttunul) = **outside time**.
- The cycle is multi-levelled: also a story about journey from land to sea and back again.
