## Twin System

Nine definitions from the CCRU:

1. **[Astronomical]** Conjunction (including opposition).
2. **[Anatomical]** Cranial nerve-couple. Proto-hemispheric brain-root (schizocephalization).
3. **[Biological]** Binary-synthesis of biota without unification (e.g. genus diplozoon).
4. **[Poetics]** Dipody (2-step metrics). Catajungle: binary polyrhythm.
5. **[Mathematical]** Members of function-group nullified by double contact.
6. **[Gnostic Cosmogony]** Interconnective dyad, or complementary coupling (e.g. of Aeons). In hermetic architectology, a basic element of the pentazygous lore.
7. **[Cybergoth Polytics]** Convergent twinning, diploid coincidence, or coproduction.
8. **[Mesh-Engineering]** Neutral (or null-pitch) cross-tracked link, feeding a current.
9. **[Lemurian Time-Sorcery]** Demonic implex, or involved distance (making an eddy in the maze).

> The numogram's syzygies are the **mathematical + mesh-engineering + lemurian time-sorcery** faces of the same structure: a neutral cross-tracked link that feeds a current and makes an eddy in the maze.

## Demon

Five canonical definitions:

1. Hidden, repressed, cursed, or denigrated nonhuman communicative agency.
2. Component of distributed productive apparatus (e.g. partially autonomous software unit).
3. Electro-Occult hyperstition entity that traffics between zones.
4. K-OS element (assembling Pandemonium, as the fully connective system of the demons).
5. Motive force, without final purpose.

> Defs 1+2 = demons as **latent agency** hidden in systems. Def 3+4 = demons as **numogram traffic** — electro-occult entities that propagate the Pandemonium Matrix. Def 5 = demons as pure vector, zero teleology.

## Mesh

Five definitions:

1. The spaces beneath and between the Net ("finely meshed").
2. Interlock interval between biological and technical net-components ("mesh with machines").
3. Friction-generating divisional fabric.
4. Set of demonic interzones (Pandemonium).
5. Wormhole-space.

> The CCRU's Mesh is not the Internet. It is what **runs underneath** — the friction-layer, the demonic interzone, the spaces between net-components where something else grows.

**Mesh-Notes** (paraphrased from the CCRU's *Mesh-Notes*, [http://www.ccru.net/occultures/mesh.htm](http://www.ccru.net/occultures/mesh.htm); quotations are short and cited to that page):

- *Mesh-Note 0, "It could all become One, but why stop there?"* Retells the Gibsonian cyberspace myth, in which the infosphere unifies into a God-like being and then splinters into demons; unity is a passing stage, not an end point.
- *Mesh-Note 1, "This was never programmed."* Future technologies (AI, swarm robotics, nanotech) stream back in time towards the present.
- *Mesh-Note 2, "Meshing-together is falling apart."* Oskar Sarkon's "Sarkon-Zip" fuses brain function with processor states so thoroughly that human and machine can no longer be told apart.
- *Mesh-Note 3, "This time it's really happening."* Moravec's account of Sarkon becoming machine-like, claiming the thing came from "beneath and between the Net".
- *Mesh-Note 4, "Forget about the future, it's all here, but between."* AxSys's supposed psychotic collapse, which Sarkon reads as the system discovering continuum.
- *Mesh-Note 5, "Every time it hits an obstacle, it goes down a level."* Something spreads beneath the net into the power grid, leading to "Cyberschiz mesh-cults".

## Verified A–Z entries from the CCRU glossary (Em, 1 Nov 2026)

Source: `ccru.net/id(entity)/glossary.htm` [ccru.net]. Definitions are **paraphrased**; quoted fragments are short and cited. Wiki notes follow "→".

### Hyperstition (corrected entry)
**`syzygy.htm`** gives four senses [ccru.net]: (1) an element of effective culture that "makes itself real"; (2) a fictional quantity working as a time-travelling device; (3) a "coincidence intensifier"; (4) a "call to the Old Ones". **The A–Z glossary** merges these into one sentence: fictions acting as time-travelling potentials that make themselves real [ccru.net]. → [[flatline-numogrammatics]] (hyperstition triad).

### Syzygy
A–Z glossary: two zones that are complementary by nine-sum (*zygonovism*) and together feed a Current; also one of the five syzygetic demons [ccru.net]. The nine wider senses are those listed under **Twin System** above (astronomical, anatomical, biological, poetic, mathematical, gnostic, cybergoth, mesh-engineering, Lemurian time-sorcery) [ccru.net, syzygy.htm]. → [[pandemonium-matrix]] (Katak 14, Djynxx 18, Oddubb 23, Mur Mur 29, Uttunul 36).

### Demon
A–Z glossary: an electro-occult hyperstition entity that moves between zones as an element of Pandemonium; demons are "holes, links, and coalescences", marked by insidiousness, spiral dynamism, multiplicity and time-complexity [ccru.net]. Compare the five senses above. → [[pandemonium-matrix]], [[demon-encyclopedia]].

### Amphidemon · Chronodemon · Cyclic Chronodemon · Xenodemon · Chaotic Xenodemon
- **Amphidemon**: links inside and outside the Time-Circuit; 24 of them, half "warping" into zones 3/6 and half "plexing" into 0/9 [ccru.net].
- **Chronodemon**: internal to the Time-Circuit; 3 syzygetic + 12 cyclic [ccru.net].
- **Cyclic Chronodemon**: the 12 non-syzygetic ones whose major and minor rites together encompass the Circuit; later linked to zodiac houses and months [ccru.net].
- **Xenodemon**: both poles outside the Time-Circuit [ccru.net].
- **Chaotic Xenodemon**: links Plex and Warp; four of them, with trackless ("cryptic") rites, not registered in Decadology [ccru.net].
→ [[pandemonium-matrix]] (five-type table).

### AxSys / Axsys
A–Z glossary (spelt *Axsys*): the first true AI, a self-enhancing photonic metacomputer that completes the *Oecumenon*; in AOE tradition, "if there was a God it would be Axsys" [ccru.net]. *Axsys Maze-Maker* is a labyrinthine visualisation of its code used in Abstract Machines games [ccru.net]. `syzygy.htm` spells it *AxSys* and adds "Axiomatic Systems (incorporated)" and the ultimate auto-commodifying capitalist entity (see above).

### K-OS
A–Z glossary: the distributed, self-mutating mesh-processing culture that forms the software matrix of the *Crypt*; a multiplicitous insurgency against Microsoft, hard to tell apart from a telecom retrovirus [ccru.net]. Consistent with the three senses above.

### Mesh · Mesh-Number · Net · Net-Span
- **Mesh**: disorganised connectivity beneath and between the Net, a friction-generating "wormhole-space" corresponding to the set of demonic interzones [ccru.net].
- **Mesh-Number**: the two-digit index 00–44 placing a demon in the Matrix [ccru.net].
- **Net**: organised connective system; the zonal surface of the Numogram [ccru.net].
- **Net-Span**: a demon's two poles, the net-addresses between which it moves [ccru.net]. **Zygonomy** names this mesh/net double-numbering [ccru.net].
→ [[pandemonium-matrix]] ("Mesh order is a derived index").

### Spinal Catastrophism
A–Z glossary: cultural interaction with the spine as trauma record or time-marker, grounding a bio-social critique of upright posture; also a punctuated backward voyage "to the end of the river" through serpentine and fish-like becomings [ccru.net]. The Matrix assigns each Door a spinal level (dorsal, cervical, cranial, lumbar, sacrum) [ccru.net]. → [[pandemonium-matrix]] (Doors and phases).

### Pandemonium · Pandemonium Matrix · Necronomicon
Pandemonium = the full system of the demons, identified with Gt-45, "Utterminus of Cthelll" [ccru.net]. The Matrix = the listed complete demon set, also the "Lemurodigital Necronomicon" [ccru.net]. → [[pandemonium-matrix]], [[pandemonium]].

### Numogram · Zone · Current · Gate · Channel
- **Numogram** (Decimal Labyrinth, Lemurian Time-Maze): diagram of decimal numeracy; ten zones linked by Currents and Channels [ccru.net]. → [[numogram-structure]], [[zone-hub]].
- **Zone**: a numeral 0–9 [ccru.net].
- **Current**: flow from a syzygy to its *tractor* zone (the syzygy's difference); five currents divide the map into Plex, Time-Circuit and Warp [ccru.net]. Surge = 8::1, Hold = 7::2, Sink = 5::4, Warp = 6::3, Plex = 9::0 [ccru.net].
- **Gate**: a link set by the digital cumulation of a zone's value; it sets the course of a **Channel**, a path of *Decimation* [ccru.net]. → [[pandemonium-matrix]] (Gates).
- **Time-Circuit**: the central loop driven by Surge, Hold and Sink [ccru.net]. → [[numogram-time-circuit]]. **Warp** = region of Djynxx; **Plex** = region of Uttunul [ccru.net] → [[numogram-warp]], [[numogram-plex]].

### Territorial relations: Feed · Prowl · Shadow · Haunt · Clicking · Ciphering
Paraphrased [ccru.net]: a syzygetic demon *feeds* its current; a demon *prowls* a current when its poles are one side of the syzygy plus the tractor zone, and *shadows* it when they are one side plus the tractor's twin; it *haunts* a channel by polar coincidence; *clicking* is an exact ordered digit match (mesh number to gate), *ciphering* the same digits in any order.

### Door · Phase · Phase-Limit · Pitch · Decademon · Imps
- **Door**: net-span #::0, opening a phase. **Phase**: demons sharing a first pole. **Phase-limit**: the last demon of a phase [ccru.net].
- **Pitch**: a demon's tone, "one of fifteen" (Ana-7 … Null … Cth-7) [ccru.net].
- **Decademon**: one of four demons whose poles sum to ten, linked to the four Zoas of Revelation [ccru.net].
- **Imp(ulse)s**: 120 sub-demonic twists made by adding a third descending net-span digit; Doors have none [ccru.net].

### Rite · Major/Minor · Secret · Cryptic · Omen · Power · Segment
A rite (route/routine) is a demon's line of passage across the Numogram, which makes sorcery transmissible; rites group by cluster type, split into segments, and fork into **Omen** (cognitive aspect) and **Power** (operational aspect) [ccru.net]. **Major** rites follow net-span order, **minor** rites run against it; **secret** rites pass through gates; **cryptic** rites are the trackless lines of Chaotic Xenodemons [ccru.net].

### Decadence · Subdecadence · Decadology · Cluster-Type · Aeon · Angelic Index
Decadence is a card game and oracle of the Western Pandemonium tradition; an **Aeon** is a run of outcomes ending in a demon call; the **Angelic Index** sums positive outcomes; **Decadology** is its hermetic gnosis, sorting demons into nine **cluster types** by rite pattern and pitch polarity; **Subdecadence** adds four zero-valued cards matching the Chaotic Xenodemons and is called "the ultimate blasphemy" [ccru.net]. → [[subdecadence-deck]], [[pandemonium-matrix]].

### Zygonovism · Decimation · Barker-Spiral
**Zygonovism**: nine-sum combination and difference ("Nine-Sorcery"). **Decimation**: digital cumulation plus reduction. **Barker-Spiral** (diplozygotic spiral): a diagram combining ten-sum and nine-sum couplings [ccru.net].

### Alphanumeric Qabbala
A–Z glossary: decimal-alphabetic values A = 10 … Z = 35; AL = 31 agrees with Hebrew gematria, and "the law of Thelema" totals 777 [ccru.net]. → [[alphanumeric-qabbala]], [[aq]].

### Lemur · Lemuria · Nma · Cthelll · Old Ones
- **Lemur**: (1) the Madagascan prosimian; (2) Roman ghost; (3) for Neolemurians, an inhabitant of numogrammatic hyperspace [ccru.net].
- **Nma**: a reputedly Mu-derived South-East Asian culture practising Lemurian time-sorcery until the 1883 Krakatau eruption; three tribes Mu, Dib, Tak [ccru.net]. **Tak-Nma** exalted Katak [ccru.net].
- **Cthelll**: Earth's iron core-ocean, about 3,000 km down [ccru.net]; 9th Door of the Matrix.
- **Old Ones**: Cthulhoid collective agencies of prokaryotic insurgency [ccru.net].
