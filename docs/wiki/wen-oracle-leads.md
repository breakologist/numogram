# Benebell Wen, *I Ching, the Oracle*: reading leads

This is a reading list made from the book's Contents, List of Illustrations and List of Tables (pp. xiii–xx and following), photographed by the user. The Contents give page numbers; the figure and table lists do not, so those are cited by number only. The titles are as printed. This page lists leads only; nothing here has been read beyond the titles. Her hexagram 47 chapter is already used on [[hexagram-47-translations]].

## Calendar / gua qi ([[reading-2026-10-18]] onwards; `cli/calendar/numogram_calendar.py`)

- **Ch. 3, "Gua Qi: Theoretical Study of Yin and Yang", p. 45**. This is the main lead for our Meng Xi gua qi overlay.
- Table 3.1 *The Twelve Hexagrams of the Son of Heaven 天子卦 (Tiānzǐ Guà)*. These are the 12 sovereign (消息) hexagrams; compare Bastian's "Sovereign Hexagrams" 24, 19, 11, 34, 43, 1, 44, 33, 12, 20, 23, 2 ([[bastian-before-completion]]).
- Table 3.2 *The Four Perfected Hexagrams (四正卦, Sì Zhèng Guà) and the Twenty-Four Solar Terms*. These correspond to our `CARDINAL` 坎震離兌, one line per solar term.
- Table 3.3 *Gua Qi Diagram Attributed to Confucius*. Fig. 3.6 *Gua Qi yin and yang fluctuations*.
- Table 4.13 *Shao Yong's Nine Categories of Time*.
- Table 7.6 *Solar Terms and Lunar Months of Spring*; 7.7 *Plum Blossom Calendar Numerology Formulas*; 7.8 *Numerology of the Year (Stems and Branches)*; 7.9 *… of the Hour (Earthly Branches)*; 7.10 *… of Zodiac Signs (Ascendant Hour)*.
- Tables 12.1–12.6: *Solar Terms to Solar Longitude and Lunar Months*; *Solar Terms, Eight Trigrams, and the Lo Shu*; *Solar Terms and Taiji Trigram Systems* Parts 1–2; *Heavenly Stems, Trigrams, and Wu Xing*; *Earthly Branches, Trigrams, and Wu Xing*.
- **Appendix A: Twenty-Four Solar Terms, p. 831**; **Appendix B: Stems, Branches, and Trigrams, p. 837**. Fig. 4.6 *The sexagenary cycle*.

## Tai Xuan / Em ([[em-state-analysis]]; `cli/taixuan.py`)

- **Ch. 3, "Esoteric I Ching: Yi wei and the School of Mystery", p. 42**. The *Yi wei* (apocrypha) are also the source of our gua qi order (易緯稽覽圖).
- **Table 7.13 *The Ninth Trigram Revealed (the Phantom "Xuán" Gua)***; Table 7.12 *The Xuan Hexagram Revealed in the Tarot Major Arcana*; Fig. 7.13a *The ninth Gua, for marking the spirit helpers*. A ninth trigram outside the eight is a natural place to compare with the Em/Man third line state.
- Table 11.1 *Revealing the Secret Hexagram*; ch. 11 "Returning Full Circle", p. 823.

## Tarot / Crowley / court cards ([[reading-2026-10-19-addendum]], [[bastian-before-completion]])

- **Ch. 7, "I Ching and the Tarot", p. 345**.
- Table 3.6 *Huang's and Crowley's Translations of Hexagram 7*. Crowley's own Yi King text, set against Huang, whom we already use.
- **Table 3.8 *Crowley's Tarot and Yi King Correspondences***, a check for our court table. Fig. 3.8 *Aleister Crowley's Chinese cosmos and the Tree of Life*.
- Table 7.11 *Tarot and Trigram Correspondences*; Table 7.18 *Tarot and I Ching Trigram Correspondences*; Fig. 7.13b *Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot* (1911); Fig. 7.13c *La Grande Tarot Belline* (1863) by Magus Edmond; Fig. 7.14 *Sample two-card reading*. Ch. 3 "The I Ching in Western Occultism", p. 68.

## Leibniz binary / DNA

- Ch. 3 "Science, Math, and the Genetic Code", p. 74; Table 3.9 *Leibniz's Binary Calculus and the Ba Gua*; 3.10 *The Four Bases of DNA*; 3.11 *Yin and Yang in the Four Nucleotides of DNA*; Table 5.2 *The Yin and Yang Binary*.
- Figs. 3.9–3.14: DNA and RNA molecules, the circular genetic-code mandala, the square genetic code, the circular I Ching mandala, the square I Ching. Compare Schonberger and Yan in Bastian's bibliography.
