# Mythlemuria — Lemurian Unnonfiction

> *"Puzzling consistencies between rocks, fossils, and animal species found in South Asia and Eastern Africa led 19th Century palaeontologists and geologists to postulate a lost landmass that once connected the two now separated regions."*

---

## 1. Scientific Origin (19th Century)

- **Phillip L. Sclater** named the hypothetical continent **Lemuria**, "Land of the Lemurs," using biogeographical evidence from South Asia + Eastern Africa.
- **E. H. Haeckel** vigorously used the hypothesis to explain Lemur-related species distribution across Southern Africa, South Asia, and Southeast Asia.
- Lemurs treated as **relics** — biological remainders of a lost world, "living ghosts."

---

## 2. The Prosimian Edge

Lemurs are not anthropoids. They occupy the **older, distinct branch**:

> "Outside Madagascar, competition from the anthropoids has driven all prosimians into a nocturnal mode of existence."

The submergence of the scientific hypothesis creates its own puzzle:

> "Lemurs are only 55 million years old, whilst Madagascar broke away [from] African mainland 120 million years ago."

- 1987: WWF declared lemurs the **most seriously endangered primates** on Earth.

---

## 3. Occult Reoccupation

The name Lemuria entered occultism through the same channel it left science — via Blavatsky's *The Secret Doctrine*:

> "Although Blavatsky credits Sclater as the source for the name Lemuria, it cannot have been lost upon her, or her fellow occultists, that Lemuria was a name for the land of the dead, or the Western Lands."

**Key etymological pivot:** Latin *lemure* = shade of the dead.

### Roman lemures
- Vampire-ghosts, propitiated by a festival in **May**.
- Eliphas Levi: *"Larvae and lemures, shadowy images of bodies which have lived and of those which have yet to come, issued from these vapours by myriads…"*

### Blavatskyan sequence
The lost continents as **spiritual epochs** with geographical correlates:
1. Polarea
2. **Hyperborea**
3. **Lemuria** ← third Root Race
4. **Atlantis** (built from a fragment of Western Lemuria)
5. Modern world
6-7. Two further continents still to come

### Lemurian Root Race characteristics
- **L. Sprague de Camp**: "ape-like, hermaphroditic egg-laying Lemurians, some with four arms and some with an eye in the back of their heads, whose downfall was caused by their discovery of sex"
- **Rear eye** persists vestigially as the **pineal gland**
- **W. Scott Elliot**: "huge feet, the heels of which stuck out so far they could as easily walk backwards as forwards"
  - Discovered sex during fourth sub-race → interbred with beasts → produced great apes
  - Spiritual Lhas refused to incarnate; **Venusians** volunteered → taught metallurgy, weaving, agriculture
- **Rudolf Steiner**: *"This Root-Race as a whole had not yet developed memory"* — the *Lemurian was a born magician*, body "less solid, plastic, and 'unsettled.'"

### Late 20th century drift
Lemuria steadily merged into **Churchward's Mu**, drifting east until even parts of California were assimilated.
