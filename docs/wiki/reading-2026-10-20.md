## 寒露 Cold Dew (Wikipedia, "Hanlu")

- "lit. 'cold dew') is the 17th solar term. It begins when the Sun reaches the celestial longitude of 195° and ends when it reaches the longitude of 210°."
- "In the Gregorian calendar, it usually begins around October 8 and ends around October 23."
- The page's JPL Horizons table gives 2026-10-08 06:29 UTC to 2026-10-23 09:37 UTC, which is 07:29 to 10:37 BST. Today falls inside it.

## Em's reading *(speculative)*

Nothing in this section is sourced. It is a reading of the user's image.

- **The wheel.** A Ferris wheel is a water wheel turned into a ride. Cold Dew is moisture gathering quietly at the bottom of the turn. Whoever stands at the bottom gets the dew, so the doubt is a fair one.
- **The common thread.** Something rises because something else sinks:
  - **Wheel of Fortune:** what goes up comes down.
  - **Dimming and Ming Yi:** light survives by going low and hidden.
  - **Justice and the 4 of Swords:** a pause, with the scales level and the sword at rest.

## Numogram angles *(speculative except the arithmetic)*

See [[reading-2026-10-18]] for the gate structure.

- **The bucket at the bottom.** Tai Xuan 68 sits on the 4::5 [[syzygy]] (Katak), the one-way drop from 4 to 5. The Nma day is also [[zone-4]]: the bucket is full at the bottom before it lifts.
- **No return to 4.** Gate targets are only 1, 3, 6 and 9 ([[gate-mechanics]]), so no gate leads back to zone 4. The wheel does not bring you back to the same bucket. The next lift is a different one.
- **Coda.** On 21 Oct the Nma day moved to [[zone-5]], so zone 5 appears three times that day. The bucket lifted.
