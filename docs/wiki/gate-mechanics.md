# Numogram Gates (Gates & Gate Mechanics)

Gates are secondary channels that supplement the five primary syzygy currents. Their index is derived from triangular numbers / digital cumulation, and they function as shortcuts, self-loops, and region-puncture routes across the Decimal Numogram.

> **Syzygy-current-first rule:** Primary currents (c = |a − b|) always precede gates. A gate can amplify, bypass, or subplex a syzygy, but it cannot replace it.
>
> Sources: [[numogram-structure]], [[triangular-numbers]], [[pandemonium-matrix]]

## 2. Derivation (Triangular-then-mod-9)

Gt-‘n’ indexes map *cumulative triangular traffic*:

```
C(n) = n(n+1)/2
Gate index = DR(C(n))   # digital root → 0..9
```

| n | C(n) | DR | Gate |
|---|------|----|------|
| 3 | 6 | 6 | Gt-6 |
| 4 | 10 | 1 | Gt-10 |
| 5 | 15 | 6 | Gt-15 |
| 6 | 21 | 3 | Gt-21 |
| 7 | 28 | 1 | Gt-28 |
| 8 | 36 | 9 | Gt-36 |
| 9 | 45 | 9 | Gt-45 |

The nine-step cycle `0 → 1 → 3 → 6 → 1 → 6 → 3 → 1 → 9` mirrors the triangular sequence itself: Warp dominates the middle, Time-Circuit occupies the early/exceptional steps, Plex sits at the terminal.

> Because `C(n)` cycles quickly back into 1 / 3 / 6, only a sparse subset of triangular indexes produce *distinct* gate signatures. The useful set is properly sparse — emergent structure, not exhaustive enumeration.

## 3. Gate Archetypes

### 3.1 Warp Gates
- **Gt-6** — amplification / internal swirling
- **Gt-15** — turbulence recirculation
- **Gt-21** — reverse current / drift reversal

These three act as Warp’s membrane: turbulence can loop or invert direction but largely stays inside the region.

### 3.2 Time-Circuit ↔ Warp Gates
- **Gt-6** (4→6) and **Gt-21** (7→3 / 6→3) and
- the user-visible **Gt-28** (8→1) recoil

Time-Circuit contact is asymmetric: Warp is the attractor, Time-Circuit the rare exit condition.

### 3.3 Plex Gates
- **Gt-36** — Zone-8 → Zone-9 (terminal plunge; inflects the 666-beast accumulation at `T₃₆ = 666`).
- **Gt-45** — Zone-9 self-loop (`Gt-45` ≡ 9 in mod-9; threshold event; 45-demon habitat).
- **Gt-09** — 9 self-coherence at the boundary layer.
- **Gt-36** doubles as Plex entry (from Time-Circuit) and Warp termination (from `T₃₆`’s 6-heavy collapse).

## 4. Subdecadence & Gate Pricing

In the Subdecadence game, each demon click functions like an owed toll through a gate:

- Score 0 → Lurgo (Mesh-00; `1::0`, open-dungeon entry / “Door of Doors”).
- Score −45 → Ummnu (Mesh-44; `9::8`, Earth-Screams, final threshold).
- Negative scores index demons in increasing mesh, so gate traversal progressively accumulates 'cost'.

This is the mechanic mirror of sorcerous gate puncturing: each step pays a demonological tariff before crossing.

## 6. Sorcerous Activation Criteria

1. **DR alignment** — operator state / hyp state must match the gate index.
2. **Puncturing** — intentional disruption of current flow at the gate coordinate.
3. **Toll / sacrifice** — demon call or corruption cost (Warp/Plex gates are expensive).

`Gt-36` and `Gt-45` are *terminal* vectors — they dissolve or accelerate beyond return. Routine traversal lives on 6 / 10 / 15 / 21 / 28.
