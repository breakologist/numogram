# Decadence / Subdecadence Comparison

> **Core insight from the user:** *"any number can be considered in terms of decadence or subdecadence depending on its pair and what they total."*

These are not two separate systems — they are two registers of the same decimal arithmetic, distinguished only by whether the pair sums to 9 (subdecadence / syzygy) or 10 (decadence). The numogram leans Lemurian: 9 is the structural base, 0 is the "empty summit," rather than systems like the Kabbalistic Tree of Life that treat 10 as the foundation.

## The 9/10 Dialectic

The numogram does not resolve the tension between 9 and 10. It *depends* on it:

- **Subdecadence (9-sum)** produces the connectivity: without 9-sum twinings there are no syzygies, no currents, no Time-Circuit, no demons. The 45-demon Pandemonium Matrix is T₉ = 45.
- **Decadence (10-sum)** produces the crossings: the amphidemon pairs that breach the circuit boundary, connecting Time-Circuit to Warp and Plex.

> "The spiral does not emerge until both registers are held simultaneously." — CCRU / Barker

The Barker Spiral's full geometry requires both halves. The "Lemurian" 9-sum and the "Atlantean" 10-sum are not competitors — they are the two faces of decimal dizziness.
