### 1. @cybermonist’s Group-Theory Formalisation

`elena/@cybermonist` posted a modular-arithmetic formulation of the numogram:

- Working in **mod 9**: digital reduction = number mod 9 (with the exception that root 9 maps to 0).
- **Syzygies** = ordered pairs summing to the identity element (0) under addition mod 9. Each pair contains its own inverse.
- **Delta** = difference operator, generates the currents. Allows negative results by cycling around mod 9 (e.g. 7 − 8 mod 9 = 8), so delta maps from D to D.
- **t** = triangular-number generator (cumulation).
- **Phi** = two versions, one per member of a syzygy, mapping triangular outputs back to the correct pair.

- Symmetry pairs: 64−46=18, 73−37=36, 82−28=54, 91−19=72, 55−55=0.

**Wiki connection:** This is the missing bridge between `numogram-calculator`’s current digital-root algorithm and a proper group-theoretic implementation. It also explains why 9≡0 in certain operations — a convention that recurs in CCRU texts but is rarely surfaced explicitly.
