## Head and nine appraisals [ctext]

Chinese is public domain (ctext). English lines are **paraphrase by Em**, not Nylan's translation.

| | Chinese | Paraphrase by Em |
|---|---|---|
| Head | 陰大止物於上，陽亦止物於下，下上俱止。 | Yin, grown large, halts things from above; yang halts them from below; everything comes to rest. |
| 1 | 初一，止于止，內明無咎。測曰：止于止，智足明也。 | Stopping where stopping belongs; clear within, no blame — the wisdom suffices to see. |
| 2 | 次二，車軔俟，馬酋止。測曰：不可以行也。 | Wheel chocked, horse at rest: not the moment to go. |
| 3 | 次三，關其門戶，用止狂蠱。測曰：關其門戶，禦不當也。 | Shut gate and door to stop wild delusion; it keeps out what doesn't belong. |
| 4 | 次四，止于童木，求其䟽穀。測曰：止于童木，求其窮也。 | Halting at a bare tree, looking for grain — the search runs dry. Wrong place to stop. |
| 5 | 次五，柱奠廬，蓋蓋車，轂均䟽。測曰：柱及蓋轂，貴中也。 | Pillar steadies the house, canopy covers the cart, spokes evenly set — the centre is what counts. |
| 6 | 次六，方輪廉軸，坎軻其輿。測曰：方輪坎軻，還自震也。 | Square wheels, bad axle, a jolting carriage — forcing it shakes you apart. |
| 7 | 次七，車纍其傂，馬獵其蹄，止貞。測曰：車纍馬獵，行可鄰也。 | Cart snagged, horse stumbling — stopping is correct; going on becomes possible. |
| 8 | 次八，弓善反，弓惡反，善馬很，惡馬很，絕弸破車終不偃。測曰：弓反馬很，終不可以也。 | Bows spring back, horses balk; string snapped, cart broken, still it won't yield — it can't go on. |
| 9 | 上九，折于株木，輆于砭石，止。測曰：折木輆石，君子所止也。 | Snapped on a stump, jolted on stone, it stops: where the noble person knows to stop. |

Minor character variants exist between editions (e.g. 廉/廣, 䟽/疏).

**Sima Guang on 次三** (集注太玄經): 蠱 = 惑 (delusion), 禦 = also "stop"; the gentleman thinks three times before acting, because frenzied error once done can't be covered up.

## Good stop vs stuck [ctext + paraphrase]

- **Timely stop (good):** 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 — no blame, shutting out delusion, centred, "stopping is correct", knowing where to stop.
- **Stuck / forcing (bad):** 4 (stopped where nothing is), 6 (forcing square wheels), 8 (breaks the cart rather than yield).

The head supports "stopping in time is good, being stuck is bad."

## Reading 2 Nov 2026 [speculation]

- Drawn on its own Nylan days (2–6 Nov), on the eve of Winter Onset: the stop before winter.
- Six of Cups and Splitting Apart (KW 23) at Zone 5; Stoppage at Zone 8 → 7 → 5. Theme: nostalgia, don't cling, don't hang around — appraisal 3's shut door vs appraisal 8's refusal to yield.
- Zone 8 is the mouth of **Gt-36**, the Gate of Charon (8→9, sole Torque→Plex escape [repo]): a timely stop vs being dragged through.
- Severance → Stoppage → Hardness: cut, halt, set firm.
